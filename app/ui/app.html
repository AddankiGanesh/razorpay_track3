What happens when you Fire?

Each scenario simulates a Razorpay webhook (payment.failed, subscription.halted, etc.). RevRecover diagnoses the failure, applies compliance rules (stop/delay/watch), then sends the right channel (SMS/email/voice). Open Case history to see the Detect→Attribute pipeline. Fire all + simulate recoveries marks some cases recovered for the learn loop.
